The guide covers vehicle checks, coupling and uncoupling, reversing, load security, tachographs, paperwork, delivery sites, communication, cab life and many other situations drivers face throughout their working week.

About HGV Driving Explained

Passing your HGV test proves that you can drive a large vehicle to the required standard. It does not always prepare you for everything that happens when you are handed the keys and expected to get on with the job.

HGV Driving Explained provides practical guidance for new and less experienced drivers. It covers the everyday skills, responsibilities and real-world situations that drivers face at depots, delivery sites and out on the road.

Written by Mike Henderson and James Nicholls, the guide is designed to help drivers avoid costly mistakes, work safely and build confidence through clear explanations, useful diagrams and experience from inside the industry.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

About the Book

Who is HGV Driving Explained for?

It is written primarily for newly qualified and less experienced HGV drivers, although drivers considering different types of work may also find the specialist sections useful.

Does the book replace professional training?

No. The guide supports learning and helps drivers understand everyday working situations. It does not replace approved training, company procedures, current legislation or instructions from a responsible employer.

Will it include photographs and diagrams?

Yes. The book is being designed as an illustrated practical guide, using photographs, diagrams, checklists and step-by-step explanations where a visual example makes the subject easier to understand.

What subjects will it cover?

Planned subjects include checks, defects, coupling, reversing, load security, tachographs, paperwork, delivery sites, communication, cab life and introductions to specialist HGV work.

Will it be available in print and digitally?

Paperback and Kindle editions are planned. Final formats, publication details and availability will be confirmed nearer release.

Can businesses or training providers order copies?

Options for businesses, training providers and other organisations are being considered as part of the publication plan. Further information will be added before release.
