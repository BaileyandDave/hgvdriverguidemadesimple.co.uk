About the Publication

HGV Driving Explained

Written by Mike Henderson and James Nichols and published by Wonky Pencil Publishing.

HGV Driving Explained is designed to bridge the gap between passing the HGV driving test and dealing with the practical realities of working in the transport industry.

The book covers subjects including:

  • Vehicle checks and defect reporting
  • Coupling and uncoupling trailers
  • Reversing and manoeuvring
  • Load security and safe working practices
  • Tachographs and drivers' hours
  • Deliveries, paperwork and communication
  • Specialist vehicle operations
  • Professional responsibilities and cab life

Available in paperback, Kindle and Deluxe Full Colour editions.

Our Approach

Supporting Practical Driver Education

HGV Driving Explained was developed to provide clear explanations of the everyday tasks, challenges and responsibilities faced by professional drivers.

Our aim is to make useful information accessible to drivers while encouraging safe working practices and continued professional development.

By working with transport businesses, training providers and industry organisations, we hope to make these resources available to more drivers throughout the UK.
